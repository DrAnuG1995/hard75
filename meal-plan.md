# 75 Hard — meal plan

84 kg male, 31, two 45-minute sessions a day. Partner eats the same food at a
smaller number of meals. Two cook sessions a week, mostly hot meals.
**Chicken and beef only — no turkey.** Dairy is in.

### Why this isn't "low carb *and* low fat"

At 2,000 kcal with protein high enough to hold muscle in a deficit, they're
mutually exclusive:

- 183 g protein = 732 kcal, leaving ~1,270 kcal to divide.
- Fat at 45 g (405 kcal) → carbs land near 190 g. Moderate carb.
- Carbs at 100 g (400 kcal) → fat lands near 96 g. That's a high-fat diet.

So the plan holds **protein high and fat genuinely low (~44 g)** and lets carbs
fill the remainder. With 90 minutes of training a day those carbs fuel the
second session. Every meal below is built to that ratio.

### On the deficit

2,000 kcal against your bodyweight and training load is roughly a 700–900 kcal
daily deficit — about 0.7–0.9 kg a week. Aggressive but normal for a cut. Log
your weight weekly in the tracker. If sleep, mood or session quality fall off a
cliff in weeks 3–4, add a sixth ~390 kcal meal on the two hardest training days
rather than grinding on.

Your partner at ~1,250 kcal is on the low side for an active adult. Any meal
here at half portion adds ~195 kcal if she wants a fourth.

| No-cook bowl | kcal | P/F/C |

potato up.

## 4. Rotation A — weeks 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11

### Cook 1 — Sunday, covers Mon–Wed (18 cooked + 3 no-cook)

**Beef & sweet potato hash** — breakfast ×3 — 395 kcal, 35P / 12F / 33C
Per serving: 130 g beef mince (5%) · 110 g sweet potato · 80 g red capsicum ·
50 g onion · cumin, chipotle, smoked paprika.
Dice sweet potato 1.5 cm, toss in spray oil and spice, roast 200 °C for 25 min.
Brown the mince hard in a dry pan, add onion and capsicum for 5 min, fold
through the potato. Divide into 3.

**Chicken burrito bowl** — lunch ×6 — 395 kcal, 42P / 5F / 41C · cold-ready
Per serving: 150 g chicken breast · 35 g basmati (dry) · 50 g black beans ·
60 g chunky salsa · lime, coriander.
Bake the breast (200 °C, 20 min), rest, slice. Layer rice, beans, chicken, salsa
cold; lime and coriander on top. This is the meal that carries the day's macros.

**Chicken souvlaki bowl** — afternoon ×3, yours only — 390 kcal, 38P / 12F / 28C
Per serving: 150 g chicken thigh · 120 g chat potato · cucumber & tomato ·
60 g Greek yoghurt tzatziki (garlic, lemon, dill).
Marinate thigh in lemon, oregano, garlic. Roast with halved potatoes at 220 °C
for 30 min. Salad and tzatziki in a separate compartment.

**Beef & broccoli stir-fry** — dinner ×6 — 400 kcal, 35P / 10F / 37C
Per serving: 130 g lean rump · 150 g broccoli · 35 g jasmine rice (dry) ·
soy, oyster sauce, ginger, garlic, 4 g sesame oil.
Slice rump thin against the grain. Sear in batches — crowding steams it.
Broccoli in last, 2 min, lid on. Sauce at the end so it glazes rather than boils.

#### Oven schedule
1. Sweet potato + souvlaki potatoes in together (200 °C, 25–30 min), one tray.
2. Chicken breast on the second shelf for the last 20 min.
3. Rice on the stove throughout — 420 g dry in one pot, split after.
4. Beef mince in the pan while the oven works.
5. Stir-fry last so the broccoli keeps its bite.

## 6. Rules of thumb

**Swapping proteins.** 150 g chicken breast ↔ 130 g lean rump ↔ 130 g 5% beef
mince all land within 25 kcal and 5 g protein of each other. Don't swap breast
for thigh 1:1 — thigh is ~40 kcal more per 150 g, so drop 30 g of the carb.

**Weigh raw, dry.** Every number above is raw meat and dry grains. Cooked
weights drift 20–40% with moisture and will wreck your accuracy.

**Fat creeps in through cooking, not food.** A glug of oil is 120 kcal and 14 g
fat — a third of the day's fat budget in one wrist flick. Use a spray, or weigh
the oil into the tray.

**Fruit.** 1–2 pieces a day in the gaps. Banana pre-session, apple or berries
otherwise. That's your ~100 kcal of headroom, already counted.

**Water.** 4 L. Front-load it — chasing 1.5 L after 8 pm ruins sleep.

**Reheating.** Hashes and bowls: 2 min, stir, 1 min more. Rice dishes take a
splash of water first. The shawarma, burrito, souvlaki and Moroccan bowls are
meant to be eaten cold — don't microwave them.

**Freezer.** Anything from the Wednesday cook that's for Sunday goes in the
freezer Wednesday night, not the fridge. Four days is past the safe window for
cooked chicken and mince. The ragu freezes best of the eight.
